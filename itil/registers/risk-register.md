# Risk register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RSK-001 | OCR or parse error in a gazetted name, party or seat | Medium | Medium | Page-level citations; 270-row and IEC seat-total reconciliation; residual spelling risk disclosed | Open (pilot) |
| RSK-002 | Secondary seat tallies contradict the official files | Medium | Low | Publish only IEC and gazette figures; document ⚠ in source notes | Accepted |
| RSK-003 | Over-collection of personal information (POPIA) | Low | High | Public-role fields only; no IDs, home addresses, personal phones or emails, family or health; popia-review before release | Open |
| RSK-004 | Defamation from an unsourced or unlabelled allegation | Medium | High | Labels required; RoR required on every adverse item; four-eyes and owner GO before publish; no adverse items in CHG-PW-001 | Open |
| RSK-005 | A bot or agent sends a right-of-reply message without a GO | Low | High | Standing rule in AGENTS.md, the RFC and the draft template: the bot never sends; only the owner sends | Open |
| RSK-006 | ElectionSePush licence breach (redistribution / derivative works) | Low | High | Fetch forbidden; link only | Open |
| RSK-007 | Automated SAFLII access against its terms | Low | Medium | Automated fetch forbidden; manual citation only | Open |
| RSK-008 | IEC reuse terms not yet confirmed | Medium | Low | Official public records used; enquiry drafted only after an owner GO (not in this change) | Open |
