# Allowed sources

Status as of 17 November 2026 (SAST). Changes to this list need the owner's confirmation.

## Primary (can support Confirmed)
- Independent Electoral Commission (elections.org.za, results.elections.org.za)
- Government Gazette (e.g. Gazette 55741, via Gazette Finder's copy of the GPW issue)
- City of Johannesburg official statements and newsroom (joburg.org.za)
- Court judgments from supremecourtofappeal.org.za or lawlibrary.org.za. SAFLII is consulted by hand only and never fetched automatically.

## Reputable news (Reported)
News24, Daily Maverick, Mail & Guardian, TimesLIVE / Sunday Times, EWN, SABC News, GroundUp, The Citizen, IOL, Business Day, and, added by the owner on 17 Nov 2026:
- SA Jewish Report
- Southern Courier (Caxton local newspaper, published on citizen.co.za)
- Sowetan

## Party statements (Reported only)
Official party statements (e.g. DA, ActionSA). A party statement is not an official City record, so it is never labelled Confirmed.

## Link only, never scraped
ElectionSePush (esp.info/elections): link to it, but do not fetch or reuse its data (its terms forbid redistribution).

## Not allowed
Wikipedia and Wikidata, gossip sites, councillor aggregators (e.g. Counted.co.za, Mandate SA, Said in Council), personal social media and professional-network profiles.
